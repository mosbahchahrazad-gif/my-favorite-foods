Tajine Jben Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • Minced meat or chicken kefta
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated garlic
  • A small spoon of butter (optional)
  • Fresh chopped parsley
  • Liquid cream (optional)
  • Eggs, according to the quantity of meat
  • Cheese slices
  • Oil and butter for cooking

White Sauce (Optional)

  • Chicken bones
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cumin
  • Olives
  • Water

Preparation

  1. Fry the chopped onion in a little oil until soft.
  2. Add the minced meat and cook well with the onion.
  3. Season with salt, black pepper and grated garlic.
  4. Let the meat cool, then add butter, parsley, cream and eggs.
  5. Mix everything well.
  6. Pour the mixture into a buttered baking dish.
  7. Arrange cheese slices over the mixture.
  8. Bake until cooked, then brown the top.
  9. Let it cool slightly before cutting and serving.

White Sauce

  1. Fry the chicken bones with olive oil and spices.
  2. Add water and olives.
  3. Cover and cook until ready.

Cooking Method: Oven

Difficulty: Medium
