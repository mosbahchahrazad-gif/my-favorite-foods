Chocolate Chip Cookies Recipe

Ingredients

  • 150g butter
  • 80g brown sugar
  • 80g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 225g flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • A pinch of salt
  • 200g chocolate chips

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
  2. Mix the butter and both types of sugar.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract.
  4. Add the flour, baking soda and salt.
  5. Add the chocolate chips and mix.
  6. Make small balls of dough.
  7. Place them on a baking tray with space between them.
  8. Bake for 8–10 minutes.
  9. Let the cookies cool before serving.

Cooking Method: Oven

Difficulty: Easy

Servings: About 30 cookies
