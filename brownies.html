Brownies Recipe

Ingredients

  • 225g butter
  • 450g sugar
  • 140g dark chocolate
  • 5 eggs
  • 110g flour
  • 55g cocoa powder

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
  2. Melt the butter and sugar together.
  3. Add the dark chocolate and stir until melted.
  4. Add the eggs and mix well.
  5. Add the flour and cocoa powder.
  6. Pour the mixture into a baking tin.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
  8. Let it cool, then cut into squares.

Cooking Method: Oven

Difficulty: Easy

Servings: 20 pieces
