Mahjouba Recipe

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2–3 measures of medium semolina
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Oil

Filling

  • Onions
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Harissa
  • A little oil
  • Fat (optional)
  • Cheese (optional)

Preparation

  1. Mix semolina and salt, then gradually add water and knead into a firm dough.
  2. Cover and let it rest for 2 hours.
  3. Knead again, divide into small balls, coat with oil and rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Prepare the filling by cooking onions, tomatoes, harissa and oil. Add fat if available.
  5. Spread each dough ball very thinly on an oiled surface.
  6. Add the filling and cheese if desired, then fold the dough.
  7. Cook on a hot metal cooking plate (Tôle), turning until both sides are cooked.

Cooking Method: Traditional metal cooking plate (Tôle)

Difficulty: Medium
