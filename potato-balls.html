Potato Balls Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 large potatoes
  • 4 tablespoons cornstarch
  • Salt
  • Garlic powder
  • Black pepper
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Cooking oil

Preparation

  1. Boil the potatoes until soft.
  2. Mash the potatoes in a bowl.
  3. Add cornstarch and spices.
  4. Mix everything well.
  5. Shape the mixture into small balls.
  6. Coat the balls with breadcrumbs.
  7. Fry until golden brown.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 4 persons

Difficulty: Easy
