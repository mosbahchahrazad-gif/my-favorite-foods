Burger Recipe

Ingredients

  • 450g ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 cheese slices
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • Lettuce
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Mayonnaise and ketchup

Preparation

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and pepper.
  2. Shape the meat into 4 patties.
  3. Cook the patties in a hot pan.
  4. Add cheese slices on top.
  5. Cut the tomato and onion.
  6. Place the lettuce and meat inside the buns.
  7. Add mayonnaise and ketchup, then serve.

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Servings: 4 burgers

Difficulty: Easy
