French Fries Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Peel and cut the potatoes into strips.
  2. Soak them in cold water for 2 hours.
  3. Drain and dry the potatoes well.
  4. Fry them at medium heat until cooked.
  5. Remove them and let them rest.
  6. Fry again until golden and crispy.
  7. Add salt and serve hot.

Cooking Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 4 persons

Difficulty: Easy
