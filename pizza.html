Pizza Recipe

Ingredients

  • 300g flour
  • 1 teaspoon yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 180ml warm water
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olives and tomatoes

Preparation

  1. Mix flour, yeast and salt.
  2. Add warm water and olive oil.
  3. Knead the dough and let it rest.
  4. Spread the dough and add tomato sauce.
  5. Add cheese, olives and tomatoes.
  6. Bake at 220°C until golden.

Cooking Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 2 medium pizzas

Difficulty: Easy
