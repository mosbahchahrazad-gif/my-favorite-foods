Tamina Recipe

Ingredients

  • Coarse semolina
  • Ghers (date paste)
  • Orange blossom water
  • Butter
  • A pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Sift the coarse semolina to remove the fine particles.
  2. Keep the medium and coarse grains.
  3. Toast the semolina in a tajine or frying pan.
  4. Remove impurities from the date paste.
  5. Mix the date paste with a little orange blossom water.
  6. Add the toasted semolina, butter and salt.
  7. Rub the mixture with your hands until well combined.
  8. Shape the mixture into a round form.
  9. Let it cool, then cut into squares or diamond shapes.

Cooking Method: Pan

Difficulty: Easy
